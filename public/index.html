Green is open, red is taken, amber means the platform wouldn't give a clean answer. Refresh any row for a fresh lookup.

Live, not forever. Results are fetched at scan time and cached for about 90 seconds to stay polite. Availability is not a reservation.

No accounts, no keys. The checker never creates an account, logs in, or stores a scan on the server. Everything in your report stays in your browser.

Why no Instagram / Reddit / Xbox? Those platforms lock public lookups behind a login or bot wall, so a reliable answer isn't possible without auth. They're left out on purpose instead of faking a result.

Rate limits happen. A LIMITED row means the platform asked us to slow down. Wait a minute and hit its refresh button.
