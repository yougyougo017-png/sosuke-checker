    FIELD MANUAL READ ME

    Point. Sweep. Claim.

    A native desk for checking one handle across 17 platforms, with a local name forge for fresh ideas. Everything runs on your machine; the checker talks straight to the platforms.

    01

    Pick a checker

    Every platform has its own tab in the left rail, or use All checkers for one sweep across all 17. Need a fresh idea? Open Name forge.

    02

    Read the board

    Green is open, vermilion is taken, amber means the platform would not give a clean answer. Click any card to jump into that checker.

    03

    Forge a handle

    Name forge generates smart 4C, pure 3–4 letter, alphanumeric, pronounceable, leet, gaming and custom candidates. Scan a batch on one platform, or sweep any name across all 17.

    04

    Claim it

    Open the platform from a result and register it before someone else does. Availability is not a reservation.

    HONEST BY DESIGN

    Blocked lookups become UNKNOWN or LIMITED, never a fake green. Results are cached for 90 seconds; use bypass cache for a fresh answer.

    NO ACCOUNTS

    No logins, no API keys, no account creation, no server. Selection, history and inputs stay in this app's local storage.
